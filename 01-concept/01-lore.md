# Lore

Four beats. One per generation. No wiki, no appendix.

The lore is the mechanic. Do not add story that the art cannot show.

---

## GEN 0 — HIELO

Nobody made them. They were found.

2,369 cubes, cut from the same sheet, each one with a pingu inside it. Eyes
open. Not sleeping — **waiting.** The ice is clear enough to see the face and
thick enough that the face cannot do anything about it.

A pingu in ice is not frozen. It is *unstarted.*

> `nace en hielo.`

## GEN 1 — DESHIELO

The cube cracks from the inside.

Not shattered — **cracked.** A hairline, then a fracture, then a corner gone.
Water beads on the outside. The pingu has not moved. The pingu did not break
the cube. Something it did broke the cube.

This is the generation that proves the system is real. The first crack is the
most important image in the collection.

> `calienta.`

## GEN 2 — CORRIENTE

The cube is gone. What is left is water, and water moves.

The pingu is standing in it, wet, finally free, and now the problem is
different: free is not the same as finished. There is somewhere to be.

They find each other here. Two pingus in moving water produce a third. This is
the only generation where anything is born — see `05-breeding/`.

> `se mueve. hace.`

## GEN 3 — VAPOR

There is no cube, no water, no pingu-shaped problem.

The outline is still there — faint, hot, half-gone, rising. You can see where
it was. You can see that it finished.

**It is not dead. It is DONE.**

This is the hardest image in the collection to get right. It must read as
completion and arrival, never as loss or death. If a GEN 3 pingu looks sad,
the whole project collapses into a climate parable, which is not what this is.

> `hecho.`

---

## Rules for anyone writing in this world

- **They never speak.** No dialogue, ever. The deadpan is the character.
- **Humans are never shown.** The holder is the human. Drawing one breaks it.
- **Never explain where the cubes came from.** They were found. Move on.
- **No climate messaging.** Melting here means progress, not catastrophe.
  This is the single easiest way to ruin the concept.
- **No villain.** Nothing opposes them. The only obstacle is not having
  started.
- **DONE is not an ending.** It is a state. Treat it as graduation.
