# Positioning

## The gap

Every PFP collection sells you a finished image. You buy it at its final state
and nothing ever happens again. The art is a receipt.

PINGUS² sells you an **unfinished** image and makes finishing it your problem.

That is the position: **the first PFP that is incomplete on purpose.**

## Honest competitive read

| Collection | Their position | Where PINGUS² differs |
|---|---|---|
| Pudgy Penguins | Cute, soft, mass-market IP, licensing engine | Opposite end of the same animal. PINGUS² is deadpan and earned. |
| Any 10k static PFP | Buy the final image, done | Our art changes state based on holder behaviour |
| Evolving/dynamic NFTs (most) | Evolution gated behind staking or payment | Thaw is gated behind *doing*, never payment |
| Breeding collections (Axie-lineage) | Breed infinitely, supply inflates forever | Supply expands then **closes**. No gen 4. |
| Loyalty-point apparel programs | Spend money, get tier | Your tier is a public image, not a dashboard row |

**Nobody owns "your PFP is a progress bar for your own life."**

## Who this is for

- donedone.io's existing customers — doers first, crypto second
- People who like the idea that their avatar is *not finished yet*
- LATAM-first audience; Spanish-language core vocabulary is a filter by design
- Collectors who are tired of static trait soup

## Who this is not for

- Flippers. Genesis is worth more thawed, which punishes fast flipping — this
  is intentional and should be stated openly.
- Anyone who asks "what's the APY"
- Anyone who needs a roadmap

## The three-sentence pitch

> 2,369 pingus, each born frozen inside an ice cube.
> You thaw yours by actually doing things — and everyone can see how far
> you've gotten.
> The ones that finish turn to vapor, and nothing comes after that.

## Risks, stated plainly

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| "Doing" criteria are gameable | **High** | Hardest problem in the project. See `02-identity/01-proof-of-doing.md`. Must be verifiable and purchase-independent. |
| Dynamic metadata = centralisation | **High** | Be honest about the trust model. Publish the thaw ledger. See `08-tech/README.md`. |
| Melting reads as climate doom | Medium | Rigid art rule: VAPOR = completion, never loss. Never show distress. |
| Holders never thaw; collection looks dead | Medium | GEN 0 must be genuinely beautiful on its own. The cube is the brand mark, not a placeholder. |
| Breeding floods the market | Medium | Supply caps at 5,922 by construction. Published pre-mint. |
| Phygital claim logistics | Low | Trait-count = production-run. Zero inventory risk. See `06-donedone/`. |

## The hardest honest truth

The mechanic only works if thawing is **real**. If `GEN 1` becomes something
you get by holding for 30 days or joining a Discord, the whole thing is a
skin on a static collection and the audience will know within a week.

The thaw criteria are not a marketing detail. They are the product.
