## The core mechanic — PROOF OF DOING

Every pingu starts as `GEN 0 — HIELO`. A penguin suspended in a solid cube.
Frozen. Unopened. Not yet anything.

It does not stay there. It changes state as the holder does things.

```
GEN 0  HIELO        frozen in the cube         born
GEN 1  DESHIELO     the cube cracks            warming up
GEN 2  CORRIENTE    the cube is water          flowing / doing
GEN 3  VAPOR        the cube is gone           DONE
```

This is the water cycle, and it is not decoration. It is the reason the supply
math works (see below). The arc is: **solid → cracking → liquid → gas.**

A pingu's generation is not bought. It is **earned**, and anyone can see it.
A `GEN 0` holder and a `GEN 3` holder own visibly different art.

## (pingus)² — resolved

### The problem with the raw idea

`(pingus)²` compounding is unsurvivable:

| Gen | Raw (pingus)² | Simple ×2 |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2,369 | 2,369 |
| 1 | 5,612,161 | 4,738 |
| 2 | — | 9,476 |
| 3 | — | 18,952 |
| **Total** | **astronomical** | **35,535** |

Either path destroys the one thing that makes 2,369 mean something. A number
that means "the doers of the world" cannot become 35,000.

### The fix is already in your concept

**The water cycle ends in evaporation.** Gas does not multiply. It leaves.

So the collection expands *and then completes*:

```
GEN 0   HIELO        2,369  ████████████████████         genesis, minted
GEN 1   DESHIELO    +2,369  ████████████████████         one child each, bred
GEN 2   CORRIENTE   +1,184  ██████████                   two parents, one child
GEN 3   VAPOR            0  —                            nothing is born. things finish.

PEAK CIRCULATING         5,922
```

Expansion, then convergence, then stop. Supply is **capped by design, not by
promise.** There is no generation 4 because the cycle closed.

Full mechanics and the squaring logic: `05-breeding/00-breeding.md`.

## Why 2,369

It is not a round number and it should not be. 2,369 doers, not 2,500
collectors. The number reads as a headcount — a real one, specific, slightly
awkward — which is exactly how a roster of actual people reads.

Round numbers signal a marketing decision. Odd numbers signal a real count.

---

## Why this is not a roadmap

The usual version of this is a promise: *"phase 3: breeding Q4."*

This version ships the whole state machine **at mint**. All four generation
artworks for every pingu exist on day one, in the contract's metadata
resolution. Nothing is promised, nothing is withheld, nothing can be
cancelled. The holder just hasn't unlocked theirs yet.

That distinction is the entire credibility of the project.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Generation is earned, never purchased.** No pay-to-thaw. Ever.
2. **Never cute.** Flat, deadpan, unimpressed. See `03-art-direction/`.
3. **The cube is the brand mark.** A pingu in a cube is the logo.
4. **No generation 4.** The cycle closes at VAPOR. Saying "no" here is the
   product.
5. **`GEN 3 VAPOR` must look finished, not dead.** Completion, not loss.
6. Max 4 colours per piece. Rarity is subtractive.
