# donedone.io × PINGUS² — The Phygital Layer

## The weak version (do not ship this)

> "Holders get 20% off and an exclusive hoodie."

This is a discount code with a penguin attached. It creates no scarcity, no
identity, and no reason for the art and the garment to be the same project.

## The strong version

### 1. Traits are the production run

Before mint, donedone.io produces **5–6 real garments**. Those garments are
then **drawn** as the `FIT` trait layer.

**Trait count = units manufactured. Ever.**

| `FIT` trait | Weight | Est. genesis count | Units made | Claimable by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `NADA` | 500 | ~790 | — | — |
| `TEE DONE` | 300 | ~474 | **474** | those 474 holders |
| `CARGO WADDLE` | 220 | ~348 | **348** | those 348 holders |
| `HOODED TUX` | 180 | ~284 | **284** | those 284 holders |
| `PUFFER` | 140 | ~221 | **221** | those 221 holders |
| `CHALECO` | 100 | ~158 | **158** | those 158 holders |
| `MONO OBRERO` | 60 | ~95 | **95** | those 95 holders |

*(Counts are projections from trait weights — exact figures fixed at reveal.)*

**Your NFT trait is literally your size run.** This gives donedone.io:

- **Zero inventory risk.** You know the exact maximum before cutting fabric.
- **Real scarcity, verifiable on-chain.** Not "limited edition" marketing —
  the number is public and auditable.
- **A rarity chart that is also a production schedule.**

### 2. The claim is gated on GEN 2, not on purchase

**A holder cannot claim their garment until their pingu reaches
`GEN 2 CORRIENTE`.**

This is the keystone. The reason:

- `FIT` is only fully visible at GEN 2 anyway — the art and the claim agree
- The garment becomes **earned**, not purchased
- Wearing it means something specific: *this person did things*
- Claim rate is naturally throttled, so production can be batched

> You don't buy the DONE jacket. You thaw into it.

Full mechanics in `01-claim-mechanics.md`.

### 3. The sticker pack is the marketing budget

Every physical order from donedone.io — pingu-related or not — ships with a
sealed pack of die-cut pingu stickers.

Why this is the single highest-leverage channel available:

- Costs almost nothing per unit
- People slap stickers on poles, laptops, hard hats
- It is **on-concept**: a pingu stuck to a cold surface is still in its cube
- It happens offline, for free, in physical space
- It matches the art direction exactly — these are already sticker drawings

Sticker set = one per `CUBO` variant (8 designs) plus the 4 generation states.

### 4. The woven label is the shared mark

The same mark appears in three places:

```
   drawn on the pingu   →   woven into the garment   →   die-cut as a sticker
```

One mark: **a pingu in a cube.** If it works as a 12mm woven label, it works.
Design the label first and let it constrain the logo — not the other way round.

### 5. The four words as a product line

```
NACE      born       →  GEN 0 release
CALIENTA  warms      →  GEN 1 release
HACE      does       →  GEN 2 release
HECHO     done       →  GEN 3 release
```

One garment per word, released as the collection's generations fill up. The
`HECHO` piece only goes on sale **once the first pingu reaches VAPOR** — the
community unlocks the product line by doing things.

This is a drop calendar driven by holder behaviour rather than a marketing
calendar. It cannot be faked and it cannot be rushed.

## What donedone.io gets

| Benefit | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Customer record customers *display* | Nobody screenshots a loyalty tier; people screenshot a PFP |
| Re-engagement without discount emails | An unfinished pingu is the reminder |
| Honest segmentation | GEN 2+ holders are verifiably your most active customers |
| Zero-inventory limited editions | Trait count = run size |
| Free physical distribution | Sticker packs in every order |
| A falsifiable brand claim | "A brand for doers" becomes a fact once doing is logged |

## What it costs

State this plainly to donedone.io before committing:

1. **A verification function, staffed, for 3+ years.** Someone decides what
   counts as a `hecho`. See `02-identity/01-proof-of-doing.md`.
2. **Garment production held in reserve** against claims that may arrive
   slowly and unevenly.
3. **Fulfilment ops** — sizing, shipping, international, returns.
4. **The discipline to never sell a thaw.** There will be pressure to offer
   pay-to-advance. Doing so ends the project's credibility immediately.

If any of those four cannot be committed to, cut the dynamic mechanic and ship
a static collection with a straightforward claim. Half-implemented is the only
version that fails outright.
